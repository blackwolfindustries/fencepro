How to read this. Take-home here means net income with no separate owner salary line, since your books don't currently carry one — if you plan to pay yourself a formal W-2 wage once independent, that comes out of this number, not on top of it.

Gross margin and fixed overhead are held as flat percentages and dollars for simplicity; a real month will swing with weather and job mix the way your actual 2026 months already have. This model also doesn't include one-time costs of leaving the franchise itself — rebranding, new signage, possible buyout or settlement costs — which belong in a separate, one-time transition budget, not this monthly number.
